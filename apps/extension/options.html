Access you control

VANI reads an article when you click the extension. A PDF download may ask for access to that website; you can save the metadata without granting it.

Your key and most recent capture are stored in this Chrome profile. Article metadata and selected PDFs go to your local VANI service. The extension has no analytics.

Disconnect clears this extension’s key and last capture. Revoke the key in VANI Settings to invalidate it everywhere.
